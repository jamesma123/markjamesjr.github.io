About
We build the eyes,
not the chassis.

Uncrewed systems are only as capable as what they can see. Vinci Vision Systems is a Houston-based defense technology company that does one thing: computer vision. We specialize in vision systems for detection, tracking, classification and autonomous navigation, running on the platform itself without depending on a link back to the cloud, with AR and VR interfaces that put those outputs in front of the operator.

We supply vision as a subsystem. Platform builders, integrators and program offices bring the vehicle and the mission; we bring the models, the training pipeline and the edge software that turn camera input into decisions.

  • Object detection
  • Multi-object tracking
  • Classification
  • Vision-based navigation
  • AR / VR
  • Edge inference
  • Synthetic training data
  • Uncrewed systems

What we do
Machines that see
with clarity,
act with intent.

We engineer real-time vision architectures for demanding environments. Our models detect, classify and track across aerial, ground and maritime domains, and they are built from the start to run on size, weight and power constrained hardware.

Every system moves through the same loop: generate synthetic training data in simulation, train, deploy to the edge, then refine as new data comes in. The result is a vision stack that keeps improving after it ships.
